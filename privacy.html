Lumi — privacy

Lumi is Lumina Corp's Microsoft Teams app for Lumina MPP. Updated October 2026.

What Lumi is

Lumi brings Lumina MPP project chats to Teams for Lumina Corp staff. It is used only inside Lumina Corp's Microsoft 365 tenant. It relays what you type to Lumi into the project chat you choose, and sends you a message when someone mentions you or replies in a thread you took part in.

Your messages

  • What you type to Lumi is posted into the project's chat in Lumina Corp's Teams, labelled with your name and “via Lumi”. It is then stored by Microsoft Teams like any other Teams message, under Lumina Corp's Microsoft 365 settings.
  • Lumi's own service (the Lumina MPP relay, hosted by Lumina Corp in Microsoft Azure, Australia East) handles message text in memory only while it relays or reads it. It never stores message text and never writes it to logs.
  • Lumi's messages to you contain a short excerpt (at most 300 characters) of the message they are about. They go only to you.

What the relay keeps

  • Identifiers and times: your Microsoft Entra object id, the id of your chat with Lumi, which Lumi messages point at which project chat thread, and when things happened.
  • Your Lumi preferences: whether you get messages about thread replies, and the projects you muted.
  • For about ten minutes after you pick a project, which project you picked, so your next messages can go there.
  • Project codes and names from Lumina MPP. Never money, notes, descriptions or files.

These records are kept only as long as Lumi needs them: the project choice expires after ten minutes, the records behind Lumi's messages after 30 days, and the rest is removed when it no longer applies. Diagnostic logs contain no message text, names or tokens and are kept for 30 days.

Who can see project chats

Every Lumina Corp staff member can read and post in Lumina MPP project chats, through Lumina MPP and through Lumi. The owners of the Lumina MPP team in Teams and Lumina Corp's Microsoft 365 compliance tools can see them as they can any Teams channel.

Your choices

  • Type settings to Lumi, or use Lumina MPP's Settings › Connections › Microsoft Teams, to turn off messages about thread replies or mute a project. Messages about @mentions stay on.
  • You can block or remove Lumi in Teams; Lumi then sends you nothing.
  • Lumi is not used for advertising and shares nothing with anyone outside Lumina Corp and Microsoft.
