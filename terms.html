Lumi — terms of use

Lumi is Lumina Corp's internal Microsoft Teams app for Lumina MPP. Updated October 2026.

Who may use Lumi

Lumi is for Lumina Corp staff, inside Lumina Corp's Microsoft 365 tenant, as part of their work with Lumina MPP. It is provided by Lumina Corp; it is not a public service.

Using Lumi

  • What you post through Lumi appears in a Lumina MPP project chat under your name, “via Lumi”, and every staff member can read it. Post only what you would post in Lumina MPP.
  • Lumina Corp's policies for workplace communication, confidentiality and Microsoft 365 apply to everything you post through Lumi.
  • Don't use Lumi to post money details, finance documents, passwords or other secrets.
  • Lumi posts only after you have chosen a project; check the project before you post.

Availability

Lumi depends on Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Azure and Lumina MPP's relay. It may be unavailable or paused by an administrator. If Lumi doesn't answer, use the chat in Lumina MPP — nothing you typed to Lumi is lost; it stays in your chat with Lumi.

Changes and contact

Lumina Corp may change Lumi or these terms; the current version is always on this page. Questions: it@luminacorp.com. How Lumi handles data is described on the privacy page.
